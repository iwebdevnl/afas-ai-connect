# Perplexity

Perplexity supports custom remote connectors on **paid** plans only (Pro, Max, Enterprise), not on the Free plan, and not inside the Comet browser.

## Steps

1. In Perplexity's connector settings, add a custom remote connector by URL: `https://mcp-afas.iwebdevelopment.com`
2. Sign in through your browser when prompted.

See the [compatibility matrix](https://github.com/iwebdevnl/afas-ai-connect#client-compatibility) (last verified 2026-07).
