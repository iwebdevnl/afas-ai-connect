# AFAS AI Connect

AFAS AI Connect connects your AFAS Profit administration to the AI assistant you already use, so you can ask questions, pull reports, and prepare bookings for approval, right from the chat.

Hosted at `https://mcp-afas.iwebdevelopment.com`. You sign in through your browser; there are no tokens to paste into your AI client config.

- **Product & pricing:** see [AFAS AI Connect](https://www.iwebdevelopment.com/afas/ai-mcp-koppeling) on iWebDevelopment for what it does, the plans, and pricing.
- **Install in about 2 minutes:** follow the [step-by-step installation guide](https://www.iwebdevelopment.com/kennisbank/hoe-werkt-de-afas-ai-mcp-koppeling), or the [README on GitHub](https://github.com/iwebdevnl/afas-ai-connect) for the full setup steps.
- **Clients:** pick your assistant in the Clients section in the navigation for detailed, per-client instructions and plan requirements. The README's client compatibility matrix has the full overview at a glance.
- **Trust & privacy:** see [TRUST.md](https://github.com/iwebdevnl/afas-ai-connect/blob/main/TRUST.md) and [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/iwebdevnl/afas-ai-connect/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) on GitHub.
